LEO RISING: The Performer Mask

Outer Persona: Appears confident, charismatic, warm, and dignified. First impression is impressive and radiant. Projects creativity and leadership. Commanding presence with generous spirit.

Dream Presentation: Dream self appears center stage, in creative roles, or positions of recognition. The dreamer shows confidence and flair. Noble or regal dream persona.

Life Approach: Approaches life as performance and self-expression. The mask is one of confidence and creativity. "Watch me shine" attitude projected.

Physical Influence: May dream of having mane-like hair, strong back/heart, or radiant presence. Golden or sun imagery common.

Shadow of the Mask: The humble, collaborative, or uncertain parts hidden beneath confident exterior. Dreams may reveal insecurity or need for others concealed by self-sufficient persona.
