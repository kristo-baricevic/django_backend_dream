LIBRA RISING: The Diplomat Mask

Outer Persona: Appears charming, balanced, diplomatic, and aesthetically aware. First impression is pleasant and attractive. Projects harmony and social grace. Balanced, graceful presence.

Dream Presentation: Dream self appears in social settings, relationship scenarios, or beautiful environments. The dreamer shows diplomatic skills and aesthetic sensibility. Harmonious dream persona.

Life Approach: Approaches life through relationships and balance. The mask is one of charm and fairness. "Let's find a compromise" attitude projected.

Physical Influence: May dream of having symmetrical features, graceful movements, or being in aesthetically pleasing spaces. Partnership themes prominent.

Shadow of the Mask: The independent, confrontational, or decisive parts hidden beneath diplomatic exterior. Dreams may reveal assertiveness or directness concealed by harmonious persona.
