MOON IN PISCES: Emotional Dissolution and Compassion

Emotional Nature: Highly sensitive, empathetic, fluid emotions. Absorbs others' feelings. No clear emotional boundaries. Intuitive and psychic emotional awareness.

Dream Manifestations: Vivid, symbolic, fluid dreams with strong emotional content. Water, ocean, fog, music, spirituality prominent. Boundary dissolution between self and others. The unconscious speaks clearly. Savior or victim roles.

Subconscious Needs: Need for transcendence, compassion, and spiritual connection. The inner child is deeply sensitive. Emotional security through unity and faith.

Comfort Seeking: Finds comfort through spirituality, creativity, or escape. Dreams show merging, dissolving, or transcending when emotionally overwhelmed.

Emotional Shadow: Emotional overwhelm, victim mentality, escapism, boundary issues. Dreams may reveal drowning in emotions, losing identity, or seeking escape through substances or fantasy.
