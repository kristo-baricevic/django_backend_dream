LIBRA SUN: The Diplomat and Harmonizer

Core Identity: Balanced, diplomatic, relationship-focused, aesthetically minded. Seeks harmony and fairness through connection.

Dream Themes: Dreams involve partnerships, relationships, social situations, beauty, art, and decision-making. Balancing acts, scales, mirrors appear. Social gatherings, weddings, or relationship conflicts featured.

Emotional Expression: Desire for peace and harmony. Conflict-avoidant. Dreams may show mediation, diplomacy, or being caught between opposing sides. The need to please others or find middle ground.

Life Approach: "I balance" - seeking equilibrium. Dreams about choices, partnerships, aesthetics, and fairness. The other person's perspective prominently featured. Indecision as recurring theme.

Shadow Elements: People-pleasing, indecisiveness, superficial harmony. Dreams may show inability to choose, losing identity in relationships, or passive-aggressive behavior. Being out of balance.
