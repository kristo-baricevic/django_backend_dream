CAPRICORN SUN: The Achiever and Authority

Core Identity: Ambitious, disciplined, responsible, practical. Seeks achievement and mastery through persistence and structure.

Dream Themes: Dreams involve career, authority figures, mountains, climbing, achievement, responsibility, and time pressure. Work scenarios, success or failure, hierarchies, and long-term goals. Structural elements like buildings, rocks, or mountains.

Emotional Expression: Reserved, controlled emotions. Duty and responsibility weigh heavily. Dreams may show burdens, obligations, aging, or isolation at the top. Fear of failure or humiliation.

Life Approach: "I use" - practical mastery and achievement. Dreams about building lasting structures, reaching summits, or fulfilling responsibilities. Father figures or authority relationships prominent.

Shadow Elements: Rigidity, coldness, excessive ambition, pessimism. Dreams may show being crushed by responsibilities, inability to relax, or sacrificing personal life for achievement. Falling from heights or crumbling structures.
