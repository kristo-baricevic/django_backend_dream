VIRGO SUN: The Analyst and Perfectionist

Core Identity: Analytical, practical, service-oriented, detail-focused. Seeks improvement and efficiency through careful attention.

Dream Themes: Dreams involve work, health concerns, organizing, cleaning, fixing things, helping others, and problem-solving. Details matter—small objects, lists, or precise tasks appear. Anxiety about mistakes or imperfection.

Emotional Expression: Mental analysis of emotions. Self-criticism and worry. Dreams may show things breaking down, needing repair, or being flawed. Health anxieties manifest as body-focused dreams.

Life Approach: "I analyze" - discernment and practical service. Dreams about sorting, categorizing, healing, or improving situations. Pets and small animals common. Daily routines and rituals.

Shadow Elements: Perfectionism, criticism, worry. Dreams may show endless tasks, never-good-enough scenarios, or being overwhelmed by details. Contamination fears or compulsive behaviors.
