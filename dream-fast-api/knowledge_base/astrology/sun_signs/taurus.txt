TAURUS SUN: The Builder and Sensualist

Core Identity: Stable, reliable, sensual, patient. Values security, comfort, and material pleasures. Builds and maintains what endures.

Dream Themes: Dreams often involve physical sensations, nature, food, possessions, and comfort. Gardens, homes, money, eating, and tactile experiences feature prominently. Slow-paced, sensory-rich dream environments.

Emotional Expression: Steady, grounded emotions. Slow to anger but explosive when provoked. Dreams may show accumulation or loss of valuable items. Attachment and possessiveness appear through holding onto objects or people.

Life Approach: "I have" - focus on resources and stability. Dreams about building, planting, creating, or protecting property. Resistance to change manifests as immovable objects or being stuck.

Shadow Elements: Stubbornness, materialism, possessiveness. Dreams may feature being unable to let go, hoarding, or losing valuable possessions. Bull imagery or themes of immobility and resistance.
