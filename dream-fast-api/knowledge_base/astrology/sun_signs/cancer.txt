CANCER SUN: The Nurturer and Protector

Core Identity: Emotional, nurturing, protective, intuitive. Deeply connected to home, family, and emotional security.

Dream Themes: Dreams prominently feature homes, mothers, family, childhood memories, water, the moon, and domestic spaces. Emotional undercurrents run strong. Kitchens, cooking, caring for others appear frequently.

Emotional Expression: Deeply feeling, emotionally sensitive. Protective instincts strong. Dreams may show vulnerability, needing shelter, or protecting loved ones. Emotional wounds and healing themes.

Life Approach: "I feel" - emotional truth guides decisions. Dreams about safety, belonging, past experiences, and emotional connections. The home as sanctuary or prison depending on current life phase.

Shadow Elements: Moodiness, clinging, emotional manipulation. Dreams may show being overwhelmed by emotions, drowning, or inability to let go of the past. Smothering or being smothered.
