LEO SUN: The Performer and Creative Heart

Core Identity: Confident, generous, creative, dramatic. Seeks recognition and self-expression. Natural performer who radiates warmth.

Dream Themes: Dreams involve performance, being watched, creativity, children, romance, and self-expression. Center stage scenarios, applause or criticism, artistic pursuits, and playful activities.

Emotional Expression: Warm, dramatic emotional expression. Pride and dignity important. Dreams may show recognition or humiliation, success or failure in creative endeavors. The spotlight—wanted or feared.

Life Approach: "I will" - creative will and self-determination. Dreams about creating something, being admired, or expressing unique talents. Sun imagery, gold, royalty, and leadership roles.

Shadow Elements: Arrogance, need for attention, wounded pride. Dreams may show being ignored, overshadowed, or ridiculed. Failed performances or creative blocks. The ego threatened.
