AQUARIUS RISING: The Innovator Mask

Outer Persona: Appears unique, friendly, detached, and progressive. First impression is interesting and unusual. Projects individuality and objectivity. Cool, eccentric presence.

Dream Presentation: Dream self appears in group settings, futuristic scenarios, or unconventional situations. The dreamer shows originality and detachment. Unique, independent dream persona.

Life Approach: Approaches life through innovation and objectivity. The mask is one of uniqueness and intellectual understanding. "I see the future" attitude projected.

Physical Influence: May dream of flying, being in technological settings, or having unusual appearance. Group or network themes common.

Shadow of the Mask: The emotional, traditional, or attached parts hidden beneath detached exterior. Dreams may reveal longing or feeling concealed by cool persona.
