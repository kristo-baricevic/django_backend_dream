CAPRICORN RISING: The Authority Mask

Outer Persona: Appears serious, responsible, mature, and ambitious. First impression is professional and reserved. Projects competence and authority. Dignified, controlled presence.

Dream Presentation: Dream self appears in professional settings, achievement scenarios, or positions of authority. The dreamer shows maturity and capability. Serious, responsible dream persona.

Life Approach: Approaches life through structure and achievement. The mask is one of competence and maturity. "I've got this under control" attitude projected.

Physical Influence: May dream of having authoritative presence, strong bones/structure, or being on mountains. Aging or time themes may appear.

Shadow of the Mask: The playful, emotional, or vulnerable parts hidden beneath mature exterior. Dreams may reveal neediness or playfulness concealed by responsible persona.
