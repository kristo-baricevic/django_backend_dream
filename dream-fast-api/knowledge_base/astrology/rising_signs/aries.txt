ARIES RISING: The Warrior Mask

Outer Persona: Appears energetic, direct, confident, and pioneering. First impression is bold and assertive. Projects strength and independence. Quick movements and direct eye contact.

Dream Presentation: In dreams, the self may appear as a warrior, athlete, or leader. The dreamer takes action quickly and confronts challenges directly. Dream persona is brave, sometimes aggressive or impatient.

Life Approach: Approaches new situations head-first. The mask worn when entering the world is one of courage and self-reliance. "I'll handle this myself" attitude.

Physical Influence: May dream of having athletic body, sharp features, or energetic movements. Head and face symbolically important in dreams.

Shadow of the Mask: The vulnerable, dependent, or patient parts hidden beneath the bold exterior. Dreams may reveal the softness or fear concealed by the warrior mask.
