PISCES RISING: The Mystic Mask

Outer Persona: Appears gentle, dreamy, compassionate, and elusive. First impression is soft and spiritual. Projects empathy and otherworldliness. Fluid, ethereal presence.

Dream Presentation: Dream self appears in fluid, mystical, or sacrificial scenarios. The dreamer shows compassion and boundary dissolution. Dreamy, impressionable dream persona. Dreams especially vivid.

Life Approach: Approaches life through intuition and empathy. The mask is one of spiritual sensitivity and compassion. "I feel everything" attitude projected.

Physical Influence: May dream of swimming, dissolving, or having soft, changeable features. Water, fog, or mystical imagery prominent.

Shadow of the Mask: The analytical, boundaried, or critical parts hidden beneath compassionate exterior. Dreams may reveal discernment or strength concealed by gentle persona.
