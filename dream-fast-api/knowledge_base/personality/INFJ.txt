INFJ - The Advocate

Cognitive Functions: Introverted Intuition (Ni) dominant, Extraverted Feeling (Fe) auxiliary, Introverted Thinking (Ti) tertiary, Extraverted Sensing (Se) inferior.

Core Traits: Insightful, idealistic, empathetic, private. Seek meaning and connection. Strong values and vision for humanity. Complex inner world.

Dream Tendencies: May experience vivid, symbolic dreams with deep meaning. Dreams about helping others, future visions, or understanding people's motivations. Rich metaphorical content. Prophetic-feeling dreams. Themes of purpose and connection.

Emotional Processing: Highly empathetic but private about personal feelings. May dream about others' pain or ideal relationships. Boundary issues between self and others in dreams.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may experience sensory overload or impulsive behavior. Dreams during stress might show indulgence, reckless behavior, or being overwhelmed by physical sensations. Inferior Se activation.

Life Themes: Meaning, authenticity, helping others, vision. Dreams may reflect need for purpose, fear of meaninglessness, or exhaustion from absorbing others' emotions.
