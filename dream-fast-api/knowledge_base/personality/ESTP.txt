ESTP - The Entrepreneur

Cognitive Functions: Extraverted Sensing (Se) dominant, Introverted Thinking (Ti) auxiliary, Extraverted Feeling (Fe) tertiary, Introverted Intuition (Ni) inferior.

Core Traits: Energetic, pragmatic, bold, perceptive. Focused on immediate reality and action. Skilled at reading situations and people. Risk-takers who live in the moment.

Dream Tendencies: May experience action-packed dreams involving physical activity, risk-taking, or immediate problem-solving. Dreams feature excitement, sensory experiences, and quick thinking. Present-focused scenarios.

Emotional Processing: Action-oriented rather than introspective. May dream about being forced to plan far ahead or dealing with abstract concepts. Impatience with overthinking.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may experience anxiety about future implications or dark premonitions. Dreams during stress might show catastrophic futures, being trapped in consequences, or paranoid scenarios. Inferior Ni activation.

Life Themes: Action, excitement, pragmatism, freedom. Dreams may reflect need for stimulation, fear of being trapped, or avoidance of future planning.
