ISFJ - The Defender

Cognitive Functions: Introverted Sensing (Si) dominant, Extraverted Feeling (Fe) auxiliary, Introverted Thinking (Ti) tertiary, Extraverted Intuition (Ne) inferior.

Core Traits: Caring, loyal, practical, traditional. Dedicated to helping and protecting others. Strong memory for details about people they care for.

Dream Tendencies: May experience dreams involving caring for others, home, family, past memories, or daily responsibilities. Dreams feature people they care about and domestic or nurturing scenarios.

Emotional Processing: Deeply caring but may suppress own needs. May dream about failing to help someone, being unappreciated, or neglecting duties. Others' needs often overshadow own in dreams.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may experience anxiety about future catastrophes or worst-case scenarios. Dreams during stress might show everything going wrong, being unable to help, or overwhelming negative possibilities. Inferior Ne activation.

Life Themes: Service, loyalty, tradition, care. Dreams may reflect need to help others, fear of letting people down, or exhaustion from putting others first.
