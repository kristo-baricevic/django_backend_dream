INTP - The Logician

Cognitive Functions: Introverted Thinking (Ti) dominant, Extraverted Intuition (Ne) auxiliary, Introverted Sensing (Si) tertiary, Extraverted Feeling (Fe) inferior.

Core Traits: Analytical, curious, theoretical, flexible. Abstract thinkers who seek logical consistency. Value accuracy and intellectual exploration.

Dream Tendencies: May experience dreams involving logical puzzles, abstract concepts, multiple possibilities, or theoretical scenarios. Mental gymnastics and what-if scenarios. Dreams may involve analysis, discovery, or exploring ideas.

Emotional Processing: Detached from emotions, preferring logical analysis. May dream about social situations they find confusing or emotional experiences they can't categorize. Feelings appear as problems to solve.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may become overly concerned with others' opinions or emotional reactions. Dreams during stress might show social rejection, emotional overwhelm, or desperately seeking approval. Inferior Fe activation.

Life Themes: Understanding, autonomy, intellectual exploration, accuracy. Dreams may reflect need for logic, fear of emotional situations, or disconnection from social world.
