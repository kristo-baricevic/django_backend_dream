INFP - The Mediator

Cognitive Functions: Introverted Feeling (Fi) dominant, Extraverted Intuition (Ne) auxiliary, Introverted Sensing (Si) tertiary, Extraverted Thinking (Te) inferior.

Core Traits: Idealistic, authentic, creative, value-driven. Seek meaning and live according to inner values. Rich emotional and imaginative inner life.

Dream Tendencies: May experience deeply emotional, imaginative, and creative dreams. Dreams explore values, identity, and ideal worlds. Symbolic representation of feelings. Themes of authenticity, creativity, and searching for meaning.

Emotional Processing: Deep, private emotional life. Dreams may process complex feelings, value conflicts, or ideal scenarios. Strong emotional content with metaphorical expression.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may become overly critical, obsessed with efficiency, or harshly judgmental. Dreams during stress might show being criticized, failing at organization, or harsh self-judgment. Inferior Te activation.

Life Themes: Authenticity, creativity, meaning, values. Dreams may reflect need for self-expression, fear of inauthenticity, or conflict between ideals and reality.
