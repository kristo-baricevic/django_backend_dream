ENFP - The Campaigner

Cognitive Functions: Extraverted Intuition (Ne) dominant, Introverted Feeling (Fi) auxiliary, Extraverted Thinking (Te) tertiary, Introverted Sensing (Si) inferior.

Core Traits: Enthusiastic, creative, spontaneous, empathetic. See possibilities everywhere. Value authenticity and human potential. Curious about people and ideas.

Dream Tendencies: May experience colorful, varied dreams with multiple possibilities and creative scenarios. Dreams jump between ideas, people, and places. Emotional themes intertwined with creative exploration. Meeting new people or exploring new places.

Emotional Processing: Authentically emotional but focused on possibilities. May dream about being trapped, losing creativity, or stifled by routine. Fear of limitation.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may become obsessed with details, health anxieties, or stuck in the past. Dreams during stress might show obsessive focus on physical symptoms, routines feeling oppressive, or negative nostalgia. Inferior Si activation.

Life Themes: Authenticity, possibility, connection, meaning. Dreams may reflect need for freedom, fear of being trapped, or conflict between spontaneity and responsibility.
