INTJ - The Architect

Cognitive Functions: Introverted Intuition (Ni) dominant, Extraverted Thinking (Te) auxiliary, Introverted Feeling (Fi) tertiary, Extraverted Sensing (Se) inferior.

Core Traits: Strategic, independent, analytical, future-oriented. Systems thinkers who seek competence and improvement. Value logic and efficiency.

Dream Tendencies: May experience dreams involving complex problem-solving, strategic planning, or future scenarios. Abstract concepts and patterns appear. Control and competence themes. Dreams may involve building systems, solving puzzles, or facing incompetence.

Emotional Processing: Internalize emotions and process them privately. May dream about emotional situations they struggle to understand or express in waking life. Vulnerability appears in symbolic form.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may experience uncharacteristic sensory overload or emotional outbursts. Dreams during stress might show losing control, sensory overwhelm, or feeling trapped in details. Inferior Se activation.

Life Themes: Mastery, independence, vision, improvement. Dreams may reflect need for understanding, fear of incompetence, or isolation from others. The inner world is rich and complex.
