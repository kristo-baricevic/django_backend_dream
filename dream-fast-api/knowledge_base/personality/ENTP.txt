ENTP - The Debater

Cognitive Functions: Extraverted Intuition (Ne) dominant, Introverted Thinking (Ti) auxiliary, Extraverted Feeling (Fe) tertiary, Introverted Sensing (Si) inferior.

Core Traits: Innovative, curious, argumentative, adaptable. Quick thinkers who enjoy intellectual debate and exploring possibilities. Challenge conventional thinking.

Dream Tendencies: May experience dreams with multiple scenarios, rapid changes, debates, or innovative solutions. Dreams jump between ideas and possibilities. Inventive, sometimes chaotic dream landscapes.

Emotional Processing: Intellectualize emotions and focus on possibilities. May dream about being trapped in routine or losing their mental agility. Fear of stagnation.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may become fixated on details, health concerns, or past mistakes. Dreams during stress might show obsessing over physical sensations, feeling stuck in routine, or nostalgia that feels imprisoning. Inferior Si activation.

Life Themes: Innovation, intellectual stimulation, freedom, challenge. Dreams may reflect need for novelty, fear of boredom or limitation, or avoidance of routine.
