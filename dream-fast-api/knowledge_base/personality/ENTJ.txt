ENTJ - The Commander

Cognitive Functions: Extraverted Thinking (Te) dominant, Introverted Intuition (Ni) auxiliary, Extraverted Sensing (Se) tertiary, Introverted Feeling (Fi) inferior.

Core Traits: Decisive, strategic, organized, ambitious. Natural leaders who value efficiency and results. Direct communication style.

Dream Tendencies: May experience dreams involving leadership, achievement, organizing systems, or strategic challenges. Dreams feature goals, hierarchies, or competition. Taking charge or facing obstacles to plans.

Emotional Processing: Minimize personal emotions in favor of logic and efficiency. May dream about emotional vulnerability they suppress or values conflicts. Personal feelings appear as obstacles to overcome.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may experience intense, uncharacteristic emotional reactions or value crises. Dreams during stress might show emotional meltdowns, feeling unappreciated, or questioning life's meaning. Inferior Fi activation.

Life Themes: Achievement, control, efficiency, leadership. Dreams may reflect need for mastery, fear of failure or incompetence, or disconnection from personal values.
