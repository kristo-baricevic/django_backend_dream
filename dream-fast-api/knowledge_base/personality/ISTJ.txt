ISTJ - The Logistician

Cognitive Functions: Introverted Sensing (Si) dominant, Extraverted Thinking (Te) auxiliary, Introverted Feeling (Fi) tertiary, Extraverted Intuition (Ne) inferior.

Core Traits: Responsible, organized, practical, traditional. Value duty, accuracy, and established procedures. Reliable and detail-oriented.

Dream Tendencies: May experience structured dreams involving work, duty, past memories, or following procedures. Dreams may be concrete and realistic. Themes of responsibility, organization, or maintaining order.

Emotional Processing: Private, controlled emotional expression. May dream about emotional situations they struggle to address or values conflicts. Duty versus personal desire themes.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may experience anxiety about future possibilities or catastrophic thinking. Dreams during stress might show everything falling apart, multiple disasters, or overwhelming possibilities. Inferior Ne activation.

Life Themes: Duty, order, reliability, tradition. Dreams may reflect need for structure, fear of chaos, or tension between responsibility and personal needs.
