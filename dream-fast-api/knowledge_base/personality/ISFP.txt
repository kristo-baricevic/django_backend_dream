ISFP - The Adventurer

Cognitive Functions: Introverted Feeling (Fi) dominant, Extraverted Sensing (Se) auxiliary, Introverted Intuition (Ni) tertiary, Extraverted Thinking (Te) inferior.

Core Traits: Artistic, gentle, flexible, experiential. Live according to personal values. Seek authenticity and sensory experiences. Present-focused and spontaneous.

Dream Tendencies: May experience sensory-rich, artistic dreams with strong aesthetic quality. Dreams involve nature, art, physical experiences, or authentic self-expression. Vivid colors and sensations.

Emotional Processing: Deep, private feelings expressed through action or art. May dream about conflicts between values and circumstances, or authentic self versus external demands.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may become overly critical, controlling, or harshly judgmental. Dreams during stress might show being criticized for inefficiency, failing at organization, or harsh external judgment. Inferior Te activation.

Life Themes: Authenticity, aesthetics, experience, freedom. Dreams may reflect need for self-expression, fear of criticism, or conflict between values and practical demands.
