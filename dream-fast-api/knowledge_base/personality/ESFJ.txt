ESFJ - The Consul

Cognitive Functions: Extraverted Feeling (Fe) dominant, Introverted Sensing (Si) auxiliary, Extraverted Intuition (Ne) tertiary, Introverted Thinking (Ti) inferior.

Core Traits: Caring, sociable, organized, traditional. Focused on harmony and helping others. Strong sense of duty to community and loved ones.

Dream Tendencies: May experience dreams involving social situations, caring for others, family, or community events. Dreams feature relationships, harmony, and fulfilling duties. Scenarios of bringing people together.

Emotional Processing: Emotionally expressive and socially attuned. May dream about conflict, social rejection, or failing to help others. Harmony and approval themes prominent.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may become overly critical, analytical, or doubt their own judgment. Dreams during stress might show being coldly logical, questioning social conventions, or feeling intellectually inadequate. Inferior Ti activation.

Life Themes: Harmony, service, tradition, connection. Dreams may reflect need for approval, fear of conflict, or exhaustion from maintaining social harmony.
