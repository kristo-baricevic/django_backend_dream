ISTP - The Virtuoso

Cognitive Functions: Introverted Thinking (Ti) dominant, Extraverted Sensing (Se) auxiliary, Introverted Intuition (Ni) tertiary, Extraverted Feeling (Fe) inferior.

Core Traits: Practical, analytical, independent, adaptable. Skilled with tools and systems. Value efficiency and direct experience. Cool under pressure.

Dream Tendencies: May experience dreams involving problem-solving, physical action, mechanics, or hands-on challenges. Dreams feature using tools, fixing things, or physical competence. Action-oriented scenarios.

Emotional Processing: Detached from emotions, focused on logic and action. May dream about emotional situations they find confusing or social scenarios requiring emotional expression.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may become overly concerned with others' opinions or emotional reactions. Dreams during stress might show social anxiety, desperately seeking approval, or emotional overwhelm. Inferior Fe activation.

Life Themes: Competence, freedom, efficiency, direct experience. Dreams may reflect need for autonomy, fear of emotional demands, or preference for action over feeling.
