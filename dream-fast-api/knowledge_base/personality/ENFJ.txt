ENFJ - The Protagonist

Cognitive Functions: Extraverted Feeling (Fe) dominant, Introverted Intuition (Ni) auxiliary, Extraverted Sensing (Se) tertiary, Introverted Thinking (Ti) inferior.

Core Traits: Charismatic, empathetic, organized, idealistic. Natural teachers and leaders who focus on helping others grow. Strong people skills.

Dream Tendencies: May experience dreams involving relationships, helping others, group harmony, or social scenarios. Dreams about guiding people or creating positive change. Leadership in service of others.

Emotional Processing: Highly attuned to others' emotions. May dream about relationship conflicts, failing to help someone, or social disharmony. Others' needs prominent in dreams.

Stress Patterns: Under stress, may become overly critical, analytical, or doubt their own logic. Dreams during stress might show being coldly logical, questioning everything, or feeling intellectually inadequate. Inferior Ti activation.

Life Themes: Connection, growth, harmony, service. Dreams may reflect need to help others, fear of conflict or disconnection, or exhaustion from caretaking.
